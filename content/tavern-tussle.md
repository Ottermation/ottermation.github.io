# Tavern Tussle

Tavern Tussle is a 2–4 player local and online multiplayer fantasy-themed party game. Entertain the tavern in frantic minigames to pay off your debt, or be turned into a frog by the maniacal wizard!

## About Tavern Tussle
{{< inline_video src="/videos/taverntussle/steamvid1.webm" >}}

Welcome to the tavern, adventurer. We’ve noticed you haven’t been paying off your tab, so buckle up; it’s time to pay it off the hard way! Compete against your adventuring party in a series of frantic minigames to entertain the tavern and pay off your debt. Or be turned into a frog. Your choice. 

## Multiplayer
Assemble your party! Mix and match 2 – 4 players online and locally. Assemble your party from those across the world to those in shoving range; they can all play together (provided you’re willing to betray them).

{{< inline_video src="/videos/taverntussle/steamvid2.webm" >}}

## Shove
Be the problem you want to see in the world. Shove your fellow adventurers at any time, strategically or spitefully. It’s all worth it to collect that extra few gold (friendship insurance sold separately).

{{< inline_video src="/videos/taverntussle/steamvid3.webm" >}}

## Minigames
Tussle your way through 15+ unique minigames handcrafted by the wizard. Be the adventurer who floats as your party members drown in debt.
